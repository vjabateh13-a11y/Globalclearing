About Us

About Global Clearing & Forwarding Firm

GCFF is a fast-growing Liberian company specialized in the provision of clearing, forwarding, transport and logistics services for clients moving cargo through Liberia’s land, sea and air ports of entry.

We use strategic insight, tailored solutions and a collaborative working style to help clients achieve sustainable results. Our team works across a wide range of industries — from corporate clients and government institutions to family-owned businesses and private organizations — always with the goal of supporting meaningful business transformation.

  • Strategic insight into Liberia’s clearing and logistics landscape
  • Tailored solutions built around each client’s cargo and timeline
  • A collaborative working style from first enquiry to final delivery
